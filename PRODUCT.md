# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A community for everyone in Israel who lives music: music students, teachers, players and singers, producers, and music lovers. They come to belong to a scene and to use that community to move their music career forward: be seen, find people, get opportunities, learn. Mostly on phones, in Hebrew (right to left).

## Product Purpose
MusicHub is a social network for musicians. Success is a member who shares their music, gets noticed by the community, and turns that into something real: a band, a session, a gig, a student, a paid job, a course finished.

## Positioning
Not a general social network and not a classifieds board: one place where a musician's profile, their music posts, the people looking to play with them, shared projects, and courses all connect, built for the Israeli scene in Hebrew.

## Operating Context
Daily use is split equally between two jobs:
- the feed: posting tracks (audio files), YouTube/Vimeo videos, images and updates; likes, comments, and "looking for partners" posts;
- finding people and projects: searching musicians by instrument, role, genre, city or region, and what they look for; opening and joining collaboration projects with members, requests and a task board; private real-time messages.
Also: musical CV with visibility control, follows, notifications, courses (catalog, video lessons, progress, Pro courses, a course editor for teachers), plans (Free, Pro, Premium; payments not live yet), admin moderation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React + Vite + Tailwind + shadcn/ui, Supabase backend, deployed on Vercel from `main`.
- Hebrew, right to left throughout.
- Pre-launch: almost no real members or content yet; empty states are what most visitors see first.
- Payments (Paddle) are not configured; paid plans show "soon".

## Brand Commitments
- The name MusicHub.
- The colors: riso blue #2F45C2, pink #FF4F81, yellow #FFD84D, ink #1E1B3A. Everything else about the look may be replaced.

## Evidence on Hand
No real members, posts, testimonials, press or member counts exist yet. Do not invent any of them in shipping UI; demonstration content in design work must be labeled as such.

## Product Principles
- Community first: every screen should make other musicians and their music visible.
- Music is the content: tracks, videos and people lead; chrome stays out of the way.
- Every connection should lead somewhere real: a message, a project, a gig, a lesson.
- Equal home for beginners and pros, students and teachers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Israeli accessibility law applies (IS 5568, WCAG 2.0 AA level); an accessibility statement page exists.

## Standing Preference
Chosen 2026-11-19: a light app at the craft level of Spotify and LinkedIn (the category standard, executed fully), keeping the MusicHub name and brand colors.
